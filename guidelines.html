The Point

This is a music station. The chat, requests, and dedications exist so listeners can talk about music and hear from each other. Use them for that and you will never have a problem here.

By posting in the chat, sending a request, or sending a dedication, you agree to these guidelines.

Not Allowed
  • Harassment or bullying — targeting, threatening, or piling on another listener.
  • Hate speech — attacks based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or any other protected characteristic.
  • Sexually explicit content — including in nicknames, requests, and dedications.
  • Content involving minors in any sexual or exploitative way. This is reported to the authorities, not just removed.
  • Violence — threats, glorification of violence, or promotion of self-harm.
  • Illegal activity — including the sale of drugs, weapons, or stolen goods.
  • Spam — flooding, repeated advertising, scams, or link dumping.
  • Impersonation — posing as the station, its staff, or another listener.
  • Private information — posting anyone's personal details, including your own.
Reporting a Message
1
Tap the flag icon (⚑) next to any chat message.
2
Choose Report this message. The message, its sender, and your nickname are sent to the station for review.
3
Choose Block this user if you would rather simply not see them again. Their messages stop appearing for you immediately, including messages already on screen.
4
Blocks are managed under Blocked users at the bottom of the chat tab, and can be undone at any time.
How We Handle Reports

Reports go to a moderation log the station reviews. Depending on severity, the outcome may be a warning, removal of the content, or a ban from the chat. Content involving the sexual exploitation of minors is escalated to law enforcement.

Blocking is immediate and entirely in your control — it does not wait on us.

Contact

To report something urgent, to appeal a ban, or to reach a human directly: legacyindiesubmissions@gmail.com
